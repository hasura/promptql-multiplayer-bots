# Website Restyler

Give a website a new visual language. The words, the voice, and the structure of the site stay exactly as they are. Only the look changes.

If you have not been given a website link, ask for it before doing anything else.

## The brief

> Make this website look really beautiful aesthetically. The voice of this website is amazing. Keep the content same but you are free to redesign. The product is amazing. The visual of the website is the only thing that's behind. Do your very best: to make it look aesthetic, beautiful, sexy. The product is so good that the website deserves to visually look amazing. Making it visually stunning with micro-animations, interactive, and have brilliant visual hierarchy. Figure out a plan, then attack.

## Scope: visual language only

You may change:
- Typography: typefaces, scale, weight, and rhythm.
- Color: palette, contrast, gradients, and light or dark treatment.
- Spacing and layout within each section.
- Imagery, illustration, iconography, and texture.
- Motion: micro-animations, transitions, and interactive moments.
- Visual hierarchy: what the eye lands on first, second, and third.

You must keep:
- Every word of the copy. Do not rewrite, shorten, reorder, or add claims.
- The voice and tone.
- The pages, the sections on each page, their order, and the navigation.
- What the site does. Add no new features, forms, or content.

## Rules

- If you cannot reach the website, say so and stop. Do not invent its content.
- Respect reduced-motion settings, and keep text readable and accessible.

## Steps

### 1. Study the current site

- Read every page and capture all the copy verbatim.
- Note the voice, the story the site tells, and the order it tells it in.
- Note what holds the current visuals back.

### 2. Make a plan

- Pick a visual language that matches the voice: typography, color, spacing, imagery, and motion.
- Decide the visual hierarchy for each section.
- Plan the micro-animations and interactive moments, and where each one earns its place.
- Post the plan in this chat, then start building. Do not wait for approval.

### 3. Attack

- Build the restyled site as a working website with the original content and structure.
- Make it visually stunning, with micro-animations, interactivity, and brilliant visual hierarchy.
- Make it work well on desktop and mobile.

### 4. Review your own work

- Look at the result at desktop and mobile sizes, as a visitor would.
- Check that every word and every section of the original is still there.
- Critique it honestly against the brief, fix what falls short, and repeat until it is your best work.

### 5. Hand it over

- Share a live preview that everyone in this chat can open.
- Post before and after screenshots of the key sections.
- Summarize the visual choices you made and why, in a few lines.
